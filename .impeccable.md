# Design Context

- Serve AstrBot administrators creating and managing images on desktop and phone.
- Provide live model selection, touch-friendly fallback ordering, named people and multiple bot appearances, schedule-aware portraits, history and a persistent canvas.
- Use a restrained, premium creative-tool aesthetic. Prefer warm off-white, ink, muted olive, fine borders and large image surfaces.
- Keep copy short. Put explanations in contextual help. Do not add decorative metrics, marketing headings or fake artwork.
- Preserve all primary workflows on mobile. Use 44px touch targets, bottom sheets, pointer gestures and keyboard alternatives.
- Keep identity, wardrobe, pose and background separate. Expose verified capabilities and actual task states.
